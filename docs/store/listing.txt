Dominus blocks the sites that pull you away — and makes going back to them
cost something.

Not a wall. A gate you have to mean to open.


WHAT IT DOES

Block a site from the toolbar, or switch on a category and block a dozen at
once. Social media, video, gaming — or categories you build yourself, with
whatever belongs in them.

When you land on something you have blocked, Dominus stops you and shows you
what you are about to give up: the streak you are carrying, and what this
unlock will cost the next one. You can stay focused, or you can pay the toll.


WHAT AN UNLOCK COSTS

You decide, in advance, while you are thinking clearly.

A cooldown you have to sit through. A passage you have to type out. Or a
Guarded Code — generated once, shown once, for you to write down and leave in
another room. Getting past your own gate means getting up and fetching it.

Unlock the same site twice in a day and the wait gets longer. There is a
ceiling on that, on purpose: friction is the point, being locked out of your
own machine is not.


ONE WINDOW

The Keep opens on where you stand right now — today's standing, your streaks,
what is blocked, and any gate you have left open with the time still running
on it.

The Fortress is where you decide what is blocked and what it costs.
The Campaign is your record: victory rate, both streaks, and a year of history
one square per day — held, slipped, or untested, because a day nothing asked
anything of you is not a day you won.


THE SEAL

A password on anything that weakens your fortress.

Blocking a site, lengthening a cooldown, raising your standards — always free.
Taking a defence down asks for the seal first, and names exactly what you are
about to lose before it does.

Forget it and there is an hour's wait to lift it. There is no master code and
no back door.


KEEP A COPY

Export your whole fortress to a file, and import it back on another machine or
after a reinstall. Importing merges rather than overwrites: it can never take
a defence down, and running it twice does nothing the second time.


HONESTLY

This is a pause, not a lock. Anyone sitting at your machine can switch Dominus
off from Chrome's extensions page, and no password inside an extension can
change that. What it defends against is you at 11pm, reaching for something
without quite deciding to.

It is built for someone who chose the constraint. Not for locking down someone
who is fighting to get out.


YOUR DATA

Everything stays in your browser. No account, no server, no analytics, nothing
sent anywhere — which is also why the export exists: a copy you make yourself
is the only thing that survives a reinstall.
