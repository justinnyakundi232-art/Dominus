    THE SEAL

    A password on anything that weakens your fortress.

    SEAL THE GATES

    A PASSWORD FOR WEAKENING YOUR FORTRESS

    Adding blocks, lengthening cooldowns and raising your standards stay free. Anything that takes a defence down will ask for this.

    This is a pause, not a lock. Anyone at this machine can still switch Dominus off from the extensions page — the seal is here to stop you doing it without meaning to.

    KEEP A COPY

    YOUR FORTRESS, IN A FILE

    Everything Dominus knows lives in this browser and nowhere else. Reinstalling Chrome, moving to another machine or removing the extension for a moment takes the whole record with it. A copy is the only thing that survives that.

    Importing adds to what is here: it merges the two histories and can never take a defence down. That also means it can't be used to roll the fortress back — a category you removed on purpose will return if you import a backup older than the removal.
